# DE CVSTODIBVS

## The forces of the shore, the lanes and the deep

The local police of the worlds, the ARCVS Astrum Guard, and the reserve fleets of ARCVS Void Command: what each is, why it is shaped as it is, and how many hulls each holds.

The hull counts here are working figures. The number of developed worlds and of sparse systems is not yet settled; where a count depends on them, the assumption is stated beside it.

The ships come from two procurement plans. The Garden is the current plan. The Old Forest is the plan before it, whose hulls still serve in the reserve fleets, the police forces and the principalities. Old Forest ships are kept in their own rows and blocks throughout.

## 1. The one page

### Three spaces

| Space | What it is | Study |
|---|---|---|
| ORBIS | the shore: a planet's orbit and gravity well | — |
| ASTRUM | system space: the lanes, the wells and the charts within one star system | astrography |
| VOID | the deep: the space between the stars | voidography |

### Three services

| Service | Designation | Owner | Domain | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local police | each world's own | the local polity | its world's Orbis and its own system's Astrum | defend its own system, and nothing beyond it |
| ARCVS Astrum Guard | AAG; also the Imperial Astrum Police | the Imperial state | the Astrum of the sparse systems | patrol and police the empty space of systems |
| ARCVS Void Command | AVC | the Imperial state | the Void, and every system through which a fleet passes | fight the wars and expand the borders |

The principalities and commonwealths, the worlds that have exited and keep the register, are not ARCVS services. Their bought ex-AVC hulls are counted in section 7 so that every hull of both plans is accounted for.

### Two plans

| Plan | Ships of the line | Cruisers | Frigates | Shore and support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garden, current | PAPILION | AESHNA | PHALAENA | BACILLUS, SALTICIDAE, COCCINELLA, TARANTULAE |
| Old Forest, earlier | — | Yggdrasil, Holzweiber | Dryad, Kodama | — |

The Old Forest's line ships now serve as cruisers. Its frigates serve as police frigates, pickets and training ships. Only the Kodama is held in ordinary.

### The ladder

An Astrum Guard cutter that meets more than she can handle calls for help:

1. A nearby member world's own forces, if one is close.
2. An AVC patrol detachment from the sector's reserve Classis.
3. A Classis squadron.

Each rung is heavier and further away. Help arrives within a few weeks at most, because a reserve Classis is not sitting in port: its ships are already on patrol across the worlds of its sector.

## 2. The size of the Astrum

The Astrum is vast beside anything a ship can reach with a weapon.

| Scale | Distance | Light time |
|---|---|---|
| A BACILLUS's guns against civil traffic | hundreds of kilometres | about a millisecond |
| A PHALAENA taking a mast | 5,450 km | 18 ms |
| A planet's Orbis, Earth-like | about 1.5 million km | 5 s |
| One astronomical unit | 150 million km | 8.3 min |
| A lane between worlds | one to several AU | 8 to 40 min |
| The outer edge of a system's Astrum | tens of AU | hours |

No weapon in the inventory covers more than a vanishing fraction of a system. A police force does not hold volume; it holds the places where traffic must pass: the wells, the harbours, the stations, the skyhooks, and the lanes between them. That is why police forces scale with infrastructure, not with space and not with people (section 4.3).

There are tens of thousands of connected stars, every one of them linked by the Corona, and only a couple of thousand developed worlds. Most of the Astrum the Imperium holds is sparse: systems of industry and stations, with populations of a few tens of thousands to perhaps a million. Those connected stars are themselves nothing beside the stars of the Imperium's zone of influence.

## 4. Local world police

### 4.1 What it is

A world's police force belongs to its own polity. It defends its own system: its Orbis, its harbours, its stations and skyhooks, and the lanes of its system's Astrum. It does not leave its system and is not built to.

A polity that is still part of ARCVS may request frigates from the Imperial state and buy them: PHALAENA, or the Old Forest's Dryads and Kodama, which are cheaper. It keeps them if it leaves. That is the exit right running both ways, applied to ships. The principalities that have left hold about 7,400 ex-AVC hulls between them, nearly all of them Old Forest.

A mature medium world covers many lanes, key points, skyhooks, harbours and stations, as the only developed world in its system. Its force is about a tenth to a fifth of one Classis by tonnage.

An average member world, between the two, carries about 25 cutters, 12 Vigils and 16 frigates: 4 PHALAENA, 5 Dryad and 6 or 7 Kodama, about 53 hulls. A few large worlds also keep an old cruiser or two; about 2,000 serve with local police in all.

### 4.3 Why it is shaped like this

Each type scales with something different:

- Frigates scale with the approaches. The outer edge of a system does not grow much as its inner worlds develop, so a world's frigates grow by less than double from small to medium while everything else nearly triples. They are the long arm: the picket on the approaches and the ship that follows a runner into empty Astrum.
- Among the frigates, the class follows the purse and the threat. Kodama hold a small world's approaches cheaply, and grow least with the world. Dryads are the working police frigate and grow in between. PHALAENA are the best eyes, bought by the worlds that can afford them, and they grow most.
- SALTICIDAE scale with infrastructure. Every new lane, skyhook and station is one more thing to watch and relay. They grow from about a quarter of a small world's Garden hulls to almost a third of a medium world's.
- BACILLUS scale with harbours and wells. Every place a ship docks or enters a well needs a cutter on station.

The harbour is the tender. A police force travels with no Coccinella; its cutters work a patrol and come home to rearm, and to have their heat, whiskers and ELYTRA seen to. The shore establishment carries a police force the way tenders carry a Classis. The Old Forest frigates draw the same kinetic natures from the same stores, because the gun crossed from one plan to the next almost unchanged.

### 4.4 What it cannot do

A world's force is large for its own system and built only to defend it. It cannot project: BACILLUS is not a Void ship, and a world's frigates are approach pickets, most of them of the old plan. It cannot stand against a war force either. Three PAPILION reach a PHALAENA's masts before she reaches theirs, open hulls at more than twice her range, and leave the cutters nothing to shoot at beyond 40 to 70 km. A Dryad is outranged further still, and a Kodama is no contest. A police force is built for criminals and civil traffic, not for a navy.

## 5. The ARCVS Astrum Guard

### 5.1 What it is

The ARCVS Astrum Guard, formally AAG and commonly the Imperial Astrum Police, is the Imperial state's police service for the sparse systems. Its officers are the sheriffs of the Astrum: the law across an entire star system of stations, claims and traffic.

### 5.2 Sector authorities

A sector authority is the Imperial state protecting the systems around a member world, not the world itself. It is usually seated at a developed member world, which hosts the authority's yard beside its own police berths. Each keeps its own.

If the member world leaves ARCVS, it keeps its own police and its own frigates, and the sector authority moves its seat. The sparse systems remain Imperial. A world that leaves does not take its county with it.

### 5.3 Its shape

The AAG is mostly BACILLUS, with SALTICIDAE as its observers, its communications and its logistic ships: about one Vigil for every five cutters. Its frigates are about 2,000 Old Forest Dryads on patrol, with few PHALAENA. It deploys no heavy ships, because it does not need them: it can call a member world's forces or the AVC, and answering that call is what the reserve fleets are for.

This keeps police power and war power apart. A service of 16 kt cutters can police a sector and cannot make war on one. Whoever commands a sector's sheriffs commands no fleet.

### 5.4 Rotation

AAG cutters cross the Corona between the systems of their sector. Most of the time a cutter is detached to one sparse system for a couple of months, patrolling its Astrum, then returns to the sector seat for refit, whiskers, ELYTRA and crew, and goes out again, often to another system.

Patrolling costs her almost no heat: BACILLUS's wings shed 19 MW continuously, so a quiet patrol never draws on her 2.4 TJ store. Her endurance on detachment is set by supply and crew, and a sparse system's stations are her harbours.

If a cutter spends about two months detached for every month in transit and refit, about two-thirds of the AAG's hulls are on station at any time. A rural citizen comes to know the rotation: which cutters return, and which crews come back season after season.

### 5.5 What a rural citizen sees

Two kinds of Imperial ship: the AAG cutter, there for the system's stations and traffic, and the AVC ship passing through on the lane. The same state, two services.

## 6. ARCVS Void Command and the reserve fleets

### 6.1 The Classis

A Classis is a fleet, usually named for the star where it is based, with a numeral: SIRIUS CLASSIS V is the fifth fleet based at Sirius. Every military hull carries the designation AVC; AVC Apophis is a PAPILION VENATRIX.

Classis home bases sit at the node junctions of the Corona, where a fleet reaches every system in its sector. One star may base several fleets. An industrial core such as Sirius sends most of its fleets forward.

### 6.2 Reserve and deployment

About 40% of the Classis are forward deployed. The rest are in active reserve: performing escort duties in the systems of their assigned sector, sending their picket squadrons, two or three frigates each, through those systems to anchor and resupply, joining them into larger detachments where a system needs more, and answering calls from the AAG and the worlds within a few weeks at most.

A reserve fleet is not idle. Its reserve work is patrol, and the patrol is what makes the ladder fast.

### 6.3 Composition

A Classis is built in one of two shapes. A forward Classis carries a battle flotilla and a strike squadron. A reserve Classis does not: in their place it carries two picket flotillas. A reserve Classis also carries an Old Forest Task Force; a forward Classis carries no Old Forest hulls.

Formation sizes: squadron 2–5 ships; flotilla 10–18 ships. A picket flotilla is held at 18 at most: when all three of its picket squadrons stand at three, its line squadrons stand at four.

The battle flotilla and the strike squadron are the forward fleet's heavy and elite elements, and a reserve fleet does not need them for its work. Its work is patrol, and patrol is frigates.

The picket flotilla serves both halves of that work. Its picket squadrons, two or three frigates each, are the patrol: they go out through the sector's systems and come back. Its line squadrons, four or five frigates each, stay together, so the flotilla keeps a line it can fight with and train in. A reserve Classis is still a fighting formation. It keeps its three task forces whole, and with them every PAPILION in their battle and vanguard squadrons.

The Old Forest Task Force holds the sector. Its Old Line is a home battle line, with Holzweiber ranging actively so the Yggdrasil can stay cold. Its Screen stands over tenders, transports and lanes, and its Dryads picket and patrol inside safe space. It keeps the old flotillas together for organisation, for distribution of the old hulls, and for lending: like any task force, it can be lent whole.

Flotillas and task forces are modular. Any of them can be detached, lent to another fleet or sent forward whole, and a Classis is built from them rather than around them. A reserve Classis usually has one Garden task force lent to support another sector, and holds its own with the rest and the Old Forest.

When a reserve Classis is called to the front, its home armada, such as Battle Armada Pleiades II:

1. keeps the Old Forest Task Force, to hold the sector;
2. assigns the fleet a true Battle Flotilla;
3. lends it a strike or special formation, such as the Special Strike Squadron.

If the fleet is being moved somewhere else, its lent task force is recalled to it; otherwise the lent task force joins the forward force. The fleet arrives with a forward Classis's full line of 65 PAPILION and a deeper screen. THE RESERVE CLASSIS carries the detail.

The Classis's BACILLUS are a precaution, not a battle force: they protect headquarters and military transports against what is not a warship, such as small craft, boarding parties, drones, or a civil hull with bad intent.

### 7.4 What the count shows

- BACILLUS is the most numerous hull in ARCVS, and PHALAENA the most numerous warship.
- The AVC carries about two and a half Garden frigates for every ship of the line, and about 2.8 counting its Dryads. Building the reserve fleets without battle flotillas and strike squadrons leaves it about 11,200 PAPILION lighter, and about 20,800 frigates heavier, than if every Classis were built forward.
- About one hull in eight belongs to the Old Forest, and about a tenth of the tonnage, *est.* Nearly all the old cruisers serve in the reserve fleets.
- About three in four police frigates are Old Forest: 13,000 Dryad and 16,200 Kodama against 10,000 PHALAENA.
- Most BACILLUS crews are rural police. Five in six BACILLUS belong to the Astrum Guard.
- The Astrum Guard holds more hulls than the navy and nearly two-thirds of its tonnage, all of it in cutters, Vigils and a few Dryads that cannot make war.
- Per sparse system: about 10 cutters on the books, 6 or 7 on station, and 2 Vigils.
- Per sector, if each developed world seats one sector authority: about 16 sparse systems, about 155 cutters and about 32 Vigils.
- Per reserve Classis, the fleets at home to answer: about three sectors and some fifty sparse systems, within weeks.
- Police force follows infrastructure, not people. A sparse system of fifty thousand may keep one cutter for every five thousand residents; a world of 40 billion keeps one for every billion or so.

The levers, if these figures feel wrong: the count of sparse systems and the cutters each one keeps. Halving either halves the Astrum Guard's cutters.
