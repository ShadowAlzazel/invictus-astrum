The AI process (1c). The CAPTAIN still runs inside the sim’s process, on the picture thread, and is only asked when the map’s clock is pictured. Two things go wrong because of that:

Pacing depends on the warp. At 10 min/s she fires every 5–6 game minutes instead of every 20 s.
Her orders land in the past. The flight runs about 30 min ahead at 10 min/s, so every order except a shot makes her fly again. At 6 h/s her turn-to-face orders get re-flown while she burns, and her salvos go out of arc.

Plan:

Move the CAPTAIN to the flight’s front edge, still in the sim process. The run-ahead worker, after flying each block, asks it about the engaged ships every engaged_s and about the rest once a block. Her orders then land in the future: no re-flies, and the same pacing at any warp. This fixes both problems above.
Then measure, and only then move it to its own process. The side pictures are built from the fleet’s arrays in the sim process, so a separate process means shipping them across every tick. That pays only if the AI’s time is fighting the flight for the CPU.
